# Chapter 0: Precalculus Readiness

Calculus builds new ideas from familiar algebra, geometry, and trigonometry.
This chapter refreshes the tools that will appear throughout the course.

## Learning goals

By the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

- distinguish the symbols $=$, $\Rightarrow$, and $\Leftrightarrow$;
- simplify algebraic expressions while respecting domains;
- connect an angle on the unit circle to its sine and cosine;
- apply exponent and logarithm rules correctly; and
- identify topics that need more practice before limits and derivatives.
