# Equality, implication, and equivalence

Mathematical symbols describe different relationships. Using the right symbol
makes a solution easier to follow and prevents logical errors.

## Equality: $=$

$A=B$ says that the expressions $A$ and $B$ have the same value.

$$
(x+1)^2=x^2+2x+1
$$

is an identity because it is true for every real $x$.

An equation such as $x^2=4$ is a condition that is true only for particular
values of $x$.

## Implication: $\Rightarrow$

$P\Rightarrow Q$ means: if $P$ is true, then $Q$ must be true.

$$
x=2\Rightarrow x^2=4.
$$

The reverse is not guaranteed. Since $x^2=4$ also allows $x=-2$,

$$
x^2=4\not\Rightarrow x=2.
$$

## Equivalence: $\Leftrightarrow$

$P\Leftrightarrow Q$ means that each statement implies the other; they have
the same truth conditions.

$$
x^2=4\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x=2\text{ or }x=-2.
$$

When solving an equation, use $\Leftrightarrow$ only when every step is
reversible. Otherwise use implication and check possible solutions.

## A calculus preview

If a function is differentiable at $a$, then it is continuous at $a$:

$$
f\text{ differentiable at }a
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
f\text{ continuous at }a.
$$

The converse is false. This is why theorem hypotheses and the direction of an
implication matter.

## Quick practice

Choose $=$, $\Rightarrow$, or $\Leftrightarrow$ where appropriate.

1. $x=3\ \_\_\_\_ \ x^2=9$
2. $x^2=9\ \_\_\_\_ \ x=3\text{ or }x=-3$
3. $2(x+4)\ \_\_\_\_ \ 2x+8$
4. $x>4\ \_\_\_\_ \ x>0$

```{dropdown} Answers
1. $\Rightarrow$
2. $\Leftrightarrow$
3. $=$
4. $\Rightarrow$
```
