# Chapter 0 readiness check

Try these problems without notes or a calculator. Then use the answer key to
identify what to review. Showing your reasoning matters more than speed.

## Problems

1. Simplify $\frac{x^2-16}{x-4}$ and state every restriction inherited from
   the original expression.
2. Solve $|2x-1|\le5$.
3. Convert $225^\circ$ to radians.
4. Find the exact coordinates on the unit circle at
   $\theta=\frac{5\pi}{6}$.
5. In which quadrant are $\cos\theta<0$ and $\sin\theta<0$?
6. Simplify $\frac{a^5a^{-2}}{a}$ for $a\ne0$.
7. Solve $e^{2x}=7$ exactly.
8. Expand $\ln\left(\frac{x^3}{\sqrt y}\right)$ for $x,y>0$.
9. Is the statement
   $x^2=16\Rightarrow x=4$ true? If not, repair it.
10. Explain why cancelling the $x$ in
    $\frac{x+1}{x}$ is invalid.

## Answer key

```{dropdown} Show answers
1. $x+4$, with $x\ne4$
2. $-2\le x\le3$
3. $\frac{5\pi}{4}$
4. $\left(-\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\frac12\right)$
5. Quadrant III
6. $a^2$
7. $x=\frac{\ln7}{2}$
8. $3\ln x-\frac12\ln y$
9. False. A correct equivalence is
   $x^2=16\Leftrightarrow x=4\text{ or }x=-4$.
10. Cancellation applies to common factors of the entire numerator and
    denominator. The numerator $x+1$ does not have $x$ as a factor.
```

## Self-assessment

- **8–10 correct:** You are ready to begin functions and limits.
- **5–7 correct:** Review the sections connected to missed problems.
- **0–4 correct:** Make a review plan with your instructor or a tutor before
  proceeding.

```{note}
This check diagnoses prerequisites; it is not a measure of who can succeed in
calculus. Skills improve with targeted practice and feedback.
```
